## Notes

- PSP renders: Phobos's headless PSP runner (tools/psp-runner) built from commit b14ef87a0, the PSP emulation code master has carried since #291, each scene started from a saved state and drawn by the Vulkan (fast) renderer at 4x internal resolution (read back at 1920x1088) on Linux, an AMD Radeon RX 9070 XT on Mesa's RADV driver. Clips: every frame captured, encoded at 60 frames a second, so a game that draws 30 frames a second shows each twice. Each PSP still and clip was measured for real 4x detail. On a still's lossless frame: the share of colour edges that fall on the 4-pixel grid, about 25% for a real 4x frame (which is chance) and 100% for one drawn at the PSP's 480x272 and shown 4 times larger, and the share of 4x4 blocks with detail inside. On a clip: the same edge share on its master's luma, every frame. A loaded scene's first frame comes from memory at native size and is never used. On 23 Oct these replaced stills and clips from the same runner that measured at native size.
- Retroid Pocket 6 shots: the device's own screenshots (1920x1080) of the Android app, Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 with Qualcomm's stock Adreno 740 driver (0676.53), N64 recompiler on (its default), the performance HUD and touch controls on screen.
- Retroid Pocket 6 clips: the device's own screen recordings of the Android app (Android's screenrecord, 1920x1080 at 16 Mbit/s, a frame whenever the screen changes, up to about 120 a second), with Phobos nightly 216022 (v2.4.0-311-g8c2b1dde), the Modern APK installed at 00:26:56 on 22 Oct 2026. Recording one started at 00:27:17 and recording two at 01:11:46, each in pieces of 180 s; the times are the desktop's clock, which the device's agreed with to about a second. The app's GPU driver setting was the system default (Qualcomm's stock driver) until 00:44:30, when its GPU Driver Manager made Turnip (StevenMXZ v27.0.0-R1) the active driver, as recording one shows; nothing changed it before Colin McRae started (about 00:51:55), and the app's log records Mario Tennis starting with it (01:14:55). Each clip was made by tools/media.py from its spec entry (1280 wide, 60 frames a second, H.264 CRF 27 at up to 2200k, VP9 CRF 40 at 1700k, no sound, a poster in AVIF and WebP); its commands and the files' sha256 follow.
- The clips' commands, as tools/media.py ran them (SRC, START, DUR, T and NAME are each clip's, below): `ffmpeg -hide_banner -loglevel error -y -filter_threads 4 -filter_complex_threads 4 -threads 4 -i SRC -filter_complex "[0:v]trim=start=START:duration=DUR,setpts=PTS-STARTPTS,scale=1280:-2:flags=lanczos,fps=60,format=yuv420p[v0];[v0]concat=n=1:v=1:a=0[out]" -map "[out]" -map_metadata -1 -an -c:v libx264 -preset slow -crf 27 -maxrate 2200k -bufsize 2200k -profile:v high -pix_fmt yuv420p -movflags +faststart -threads 4 NAME.mp4`; the same up to `-an`, then `-c:v libvpx-vp9 -crf 40 -b:v 1700k -row-mt 1 -deadline good -cpu-used 2 -pix_fmt yuv420p -threads 4 NAME.webm`; and the poster: `ffmpeg -hide_banner -loglevel error -y -filter_threads 4 -filter_complex_threads 4 -ss T -threads 4 -i SRC -frames:v 1 -vf scale=1280:-2:flags=lanczos -threads 4 poster.png`, `avifenc -q 60 -s 4 -j 2 -y 420 poster.png NAME-poster.avif` and `ffmpeg -hide_banner -loglevel error -y -filter_threads 4 -filter_complex_threads 4 -threads 4 -i poster.png -c:v libwebp -quality 78 -compression_level 6 -threads 4 NAME-poster.webp`.
